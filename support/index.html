About Enso Sudoku

Enso Sudoku is a calm, focused Sudoku game for iOS and Android. Puzzles, Daily and Weekly Challenges, statistics, achievements and settings all work without an internet connection. A connection is only needed for ads, purchases and Game Center or Google Play Games features.

There is no account to create. Your progress is saved on your device.

Starting a new puzzle

On the Home screen, tap New Game, choose a difficulty and tap Start Game. Games you haven’t finished appear under Continue, so you can pick up where you left off.

If you already have a Classic game in progress, starting a new one replaces it. The app asks you to confirm first.

To start the current puzzle over, use Restart puzzle on the game screen. The board returns to its starting clues and your time and score start again. A hint or Last Chance already used on that puzzle does not come back.

Daily and Weekly Challenges

Daily Challenge

A new puzzle every day. Difficulty rises through the week, from Easy on Monday to Expert on Sunday. Open Daily Challenge from the Home screen to see a calendar: tap today or any past day to play it. Future days unlock when their day arrives. Completing the Daily Challenge on consecutive days builds your streak.

Weekly Challenge

One Expert-level puzzle, refreshed every week. Complete it without hints to set an eligible personal best. You can play it again as often as you like.

Difficulty levels

There are six levels: Beginner, Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert and Master. Each puzzle is graded by the solving techniques it requires and how much work it takes, not just by the number of starting clues. You can choose a different level every time you start a new puzzle.

Hints

Tap Hint during a puzzle and one unsolved cell is filled in with its correct number.

  • Every puzzle starts with one free hint.
  • After that, you can watch a short video for another hint. This is optional.
  • Using a hint marks the game as assisted. That never affects casual play, but assisted games don’t count toward leaderboard times or the Weekly Challenge personal best.

Rewarded ads

Rewarded ads are short videos you can choose to watch in exchange for something useful. They are only shown when you tap to watch one:

  • Hint — another hint after the free one.
  • Last Chance — after reaching the mistake limit, get one mistake back and carry on where you were. Available once per puzzle.
  • Theme unlock — unlock a board theme permanently on this device.

Watch the video to the end to receive the reward. If you see “Ad not available right now.”, no video could be loaded at that moment. Check your internet connection and try again a little later.

Rewarded ads stay available after you buy Remove Ads, but they are still only shown when you choose to watch one.

Board themes and theme unlocks

Choose a board theme in Settings → Appearance → Board Theme.

  • Classic is always available.
  • Midnight, Forest, Sakura and Sunset unlock by watching one short video each. Once unlocked, a theme stays unlocked on that device and you won’t be asked to watch another video for it.

Theme unlocks are saved on your device. They are not part of the Remove Ads purchase, so Restore Purchases does not restore them, and they may be lost if you delete the app or clear its data.

Appearance: Light, Dark or System

Go to Settings → Appearance → Light & Dark and choose Light, Dark or System Default. System Default follows your device’s light or dark setting. The app starts in Light.

App language

Enso Sudoku is available in English and Turkish. Go to Settings → Language → App Language and choose System Default, English or Türkçe.

Remove Ads

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase, found in Settings → Purchases → Remove Ads. It is not a subscription and you are never charged again.

  • No banner ads.
  • No full-screen ads between games.
  • Optional rewarded videos (Hint, Last Chance, theme unlocks) remain available if you want them.

Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play using your store account. Enso Sudoku never receives your payment card details. The price is shown by the store in your local currency.

If you see “Pricing is unavailable right now” or “Can’t reach the store right now”, check your internet connection and that you are signed in to the App Store or Google Play, then try again. If a purchase shows as pending, ads are removed as soon as the store confirms it.

Restore Purchases

If you bought Remove Ads before — on this device or another one using the same store account — open Settings → Purchases → Remove Ads and tap Restore Purchases. There is no new charge.

If you see “No previous purchase found for this store account”, make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you used to buy it. On iPhone and iPad, check the account shown in the App Store; on Android, check the account selected in Google Play.

Privacy Choices

In some regions, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you may be asked for your advertising consent when you open the app. Where this applies, you can review or change your choices at any time in Settings → Other → Privacy Choices.

If you don’t see Privacy Choices in Settings, it isn’t required for your region. For details, read our Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Enso Sudoku can send two optional reminders, both off by default. Turn them on in Settings → Notifications:

  • Daily reminder — a quiet nudge once a day, at a time you choose with Reminder time.
  • Streak reminder — only while a streak is running and today’s Daily Challenge is still unplayed.

The app asks for notification permission the first time you turn a reminder on. If notifications are turned off for Enso Sudoku in your device settings, use Open notification settings to allow them. On Android, a reminder may arrive a few minutes after the chosen time.

Your progress and devices

Your puzzles, statistics, streaks, achievements, unlocked themes and settings are stored on your device only. Enso Sudoku has no account and no cloud sync of its own.

Please note: deleting the app, or clearing its data or storage, may permanently remove your progress, statistics, unlocked themes and settings. Avoid doing this unless you are prepared to start over. Your Remove Ads purchase is not lost — it can always be restored with Restore Purchases.

Progress does not move between devices automatically. If your device’s own backup and restore (such as iCloud Backup or Android backup) includes app data, it may carry your progress to a restored device; this depends on your device and its settings.

Troubleshooting

If something isn’t working as expected, try these steps in order:

  1. Close and reopen the app. Fully close Enso Sudoku from the app switcher, then open it again. Puzzles in progress are saved automatically.
  2. Check your internet connection if the problem involves ads, rewarded videos, purchases or restoring. Puzzles themselves don’t need a connection.
  3. Check your store account. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store or Google Play with the account you used for any purchase.
  4. Restore your purchase if Remove Ads isn’t active: Settings → Purchases → Remove Ads → Restore Purchases.
  5. Restart your device. This often clears temporary connection or store issues.
  6. Update the app. When updates are available, install the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
  7. Contact us if the problem continues — see below.

Please don’t delete the app or clear its data as a troubleshooting step unless we suggest it: your progress is stored only on your device.
