Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Enso Sudoku mobile game for iOS and Android (“Enso Sudoku” or “the app”) handles information. It covers the app itself and the third-party services built into it.

Enso Sudoku is developed and operated by Fatih Öztürkbay. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the developer. You can reach us using the details under Contact.

In short

  • Enso Sudoku has no user accounts and we do not run a server that stores your game data or a profile about you.
  • Your puzzles, progress, statistics, unlocked themes and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and process information from your device to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize ads. Depending on your region and applicable requirements, Google’s consent tool may ask for your consent or other privacy choices.
  • The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment card details.
  • The app does not include its own analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

1. Information handled by Enso Sudoku

It helps to separate three kinds of information:

  1. Data stored locally by Enso Sudoku. Game and settings data that the app saves on your device so it can work. We do not receive it. See Local game data.
  2. Data that may be processed by Google, Apple and platform services. The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform for ads and consent, the App Store or Google Play for purchases and, if platform game services are enabled, Game Center or Google Play Games Services for leaderboards and achievements. These services may collect and process information under their own privacy policies. See Advertising, In-app purchases and Platform services.
  3. Data Enso Sudoku does not directly receive. Because there are no accounts and no app server, we do not receive your game data, your name or your payment details. See What we do not receive.

The only personal information we receive directly is what you choose to send us when you contact support by email (see Contact).

2. Local game data

Enso Sudoku keeps its data in a database on your device. This data is used only to run the game and is not sent to us. It includes:

Data Enso Sudoku stores on your device
CategoryExamples
Puzzles in progressThe board, your entries and notes, elapsed time, mistakes, hints used and undo history, so you can continue later.
History and statisticsCompleted games, times, scores, mistakes, hints used and per-difficulty statistics.

Enso Sudoku has no account or cloud sync service of its own. The app does not opt its data out of operating-system backups: depending on your device and its settings, your operating system may include app data in device or platform backups (for example, iCloud Backup or Android backup) and may restore it to a new or reset device. Those backups are managed by Apple or Google and your device settings, not by us.

3. Advertising

Enso Sudoku is free to play and supported by ads served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app may show:

  • Banner ads at the bottom of some screens;
  • Full-screen (interstitial) ads at natural breaks between games, spaced out by play time; and
  • Rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one (see Rewarded advertising).

To deliver ads, measure their performance, limit repeats and help prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device. According to Google’s disclosures, this may include:

  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version;
  • advertising identifiers where available and applicable, such as the advertising ID on Android;
  • IP address and IP-derived approximate (coarse) location, as applicable;
  • ad interaction data, such as ads shown and taps;
  • diagnostic and performance information related to ads; and
  • your consent and privacy-choice status, so that it can be respected.

Whether ads are personalized depends on your consent choices where consent is required, your region and your device settings. Google’s use of this information is governed by Google’s own policies; see Third-party services.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS). The current version of Enso Sudoku does not ask for permission to track you through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. Without that permission, the app and the ads SDK cannot access your device’s Apple advertising identifier (IDFA).

4. Consent and privacy choices

Enso Sudoku integrates Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising consent and privacy choices. Each time the app starts, it checks with UMP whether a consent or privacy message applies to you. Depending on your region and applicable requirements, Google may present a consent message or other privacy choices — for example, under European privacy rules. Which messages are shown, and where, is determined through Google’s consent tools and may change over time.

Ad requests are governed by the app’s consent and readiness checks: the app does not request ads until Google’s consent tool indicates that ads may be requested.

Where Google’s consent tool indicates that privacy options are required for you, the app shows Settings → Other → Privacy Choices, where you can review or change your choices at any time.

Your consent choices are stored on your device by Google’s consent tool and are shared with Google’s advertising services so that they can respect them.

5. Rewarded advertising

Rewarded ads are always optional. They are shown only if you tap to watch one, in exchange for:

  • an extra hint after a puzzle’s free hint has been used;
  • a Last Chance to continue a puzzle after reaching the mistake limit; or
  • unlocking a board theme on your device.

Rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob in the same way as other ads and are subject to the same consent choices. The reward itself (for example, an unlocked theme) is recorded only on your device. Rewarded ads remain available after a Remove Ads purchase, but they are still shown only if you choose to watch one.

6. In-app purchases and Remove Ads

Enso Sudoku offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase: Remove Ads. It is not a subscription. It turns off banner ads and the full-screen ads shown between games, according to the app’s current rules; optional rewarded ads remain available.

  • Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • We do not receive your payment card details, billing address or store account password.
  • The app receives purchase status and transaction information from the store on your device, so it can turn off ads, and keeps a local record that Remove Ads is active. We do not run a server that receives or verifies this information.
  • Restore Purchases asks the App Store or Google Play for purchases made with your store account, so Remove Ads can be restored on a new or reset device.

Apple and Google may provide us with purchase and sales reports through their developer consoles, as part of operating their stores.

7. Platform services

Enso Sudoku includes integration with Game Center (iOS) and Google Play Games Services (Android) for leaderboards and achievements. If platform game services are enabled and you are signed in, Apple Game Center or Google Play Games may process scores, achievements and related platform account information according to their own privacy policies. When these services are enabled, the app may ask the platform service to sign you in with the account you already use on your device. If you are signed in:

  • completion times for eligible puzzles (those finished without hints or other assistance) may be submitted to leaderboards; and
  • achievement unlocks may be reported.

This information is sent to and handled by Apple or Google and associated with your Game Center or Google Play Games profile, under their own privacy policies and settings. If you are not signed in or offline, these submissions wait on your device and are retried later. Whether these services are active can depend on the app version and platform configuration. The app’s own Statistics and Achievements screens work entirely from local data.

8. Notifications

Enso Sudoku can send optional local reminders: a Daily reminder at a time you choose, and a Streak reminder while a Daily Challenge streak is running. Both are off by default. The app asks your device for notification permission only when you first turn a reminder on.

Reminders are scheduled and delivered on your device by its operating system. Enso Sudoku does not use push notifications or a notification server, and does not send reminder data to us. You can manage reminders in Settings → Notifications in the app or in your device’s notification settings.

9. Third-party services

The following providers may process information when you use Enso Sudoku. Their practices are governed by their own policies:

  • Google AdMob, Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (advertising and consent) — Google Privacy Policy; How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Google Play and Google Play Games Services (purchases on Android, and leaderboards and achievements if enabled) — Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store and Game Center (purchases on iOS, and leaderboards and achievements if enabled) — Apple Privacy Policy.

Ads displayed in the app may come from advertisers and ad networks working with Google. Tapping an ad may open a website or store page operated by someone else, with its own privacy practices.

10. What we do not receive

Enso Sudoku does not have accounts or a server of its own. We do not receive:

  • your name, email address or contact details (unless you email us);
  • your puzzles, progress, statistics or settings;
  • your payment card details;
  • your contacts, photos, precise location, camera or microphone — the app does not request access to them.

Enso Sudoku does not include Firebase, a first-party analytics SDK or a crash-reporting SDK, and the app does not connect to an Enso Sudoku backend service. Apple and Google may provide developers with aggregated statistics and crash reports through their developer consoles, depending on your device settings and their own policies.

11. Data retention

  • Local data stays on your device until you clear the app’s data or remove the app. Because Enso Sudoku has no account or cloud sync service, doing so may remove your local progress, statistics, unlocked themes and settings. Depending on your operating system’s backup behavior, device or platform backups may retain copies of some app data and may restore it later.
  • Information processed by Google or Apple is retained according to their own policies.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and keep reasonable records of it. You can ask us to delete them.

12. Children

Enso Sudoku is not specifically designed or directed toward children. Advertising and privacy behavior in the app, including how ads are requested and served, is subject to applicable platform and regional requirements. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Local game data is stored in the app’s private storage on your device and is protected by your device’s operating system and security settings, such as your device passcode. Because we do not operate a server for game data, there is no central copy of your progress held by us. Information handled by Google and Apple is protected by their own security practices. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we aim to keep the information we handle to a minimum.

14. International processing

Google, Apple and their partners may process information in countries other than the one where you live, under their own policies and safeguards. If you email us, your message is handled through our email provider and may also be processed outside your country.

15. Your choices

  • Advertising consent: where available, use Settings → Other → Privacy Choices in the app.
  • Advertising ID (Android): you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s settings (the exact location varies by device, often under Privacy → Ads).
  • Tracking (iOS): the app does not ask to track you. You can review tracking and Apple advertising settings in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security.
  • Fewer ads: the optional Remove Ads purchase turns off banner ads and the full-screen ads shown between games. Rewarded ads are always optional.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in Settings → Notifications in the app, or in your device’s notification settings.
  • Platform services: where enabled, you can sign out of Game Center or Google Play Games, or adjust their privacy settings, in your device or account settings.
  • Local data: clearing the app’s data or removing the app deletes the local data on that device, and we cannot recover it for you. Device or platform backups may still hold copies, depending on your backup settings.
  • Requests: depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws, such as the right to access or delete personal information. For information held by Google or Apple, please use their tools and contact channels. For anything we hold, such as support emails, contact us.

16. This website

This support website’s own code sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking scripts, marketing pixels, external fonts or external scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; for information about how GitHub handles data, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or the services it uses change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
